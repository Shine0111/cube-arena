## Brand & Style

This design system drives a precision-engineered, dark-spectrum digital stadium for competitive speedcubing. The aesthetic balances tournament-grade esports telemetry with clinical speed instrumentation: obsidian dark matter, ultra-crisp optics, micro-faceted surfaces, and electric bioluminescent feedback.

### Emotional Target & Voice
- **Hyper-focused & Zero-latency:** Interface chrome disappears into peripheral darkness, directing laser focus toward scramble strings, cube visualization, and the running millisecond timer.
- **Tournament Legitimism:** Eschews toy-like gamification in favor of serious competitive broadcast aesthetics—think Swiss-timed esports, telemetry telemetry dashboards, and precision chronograph controls.
- **Kinetic Feedback:** Rapid state transitions, razor-sharp state changes, and instant optic rewards when personal bests or podium spots shatter records.

### Visual Architecture
The core language fuses **Dark Mode Brutalism** (monolithic slate planes, strict 1px telemetry borders, and zero ornamental clutter) with **Subtle Cyber-Glassmorphism** (semi-transparent surface overlays, ultra-refined back-surface blurs, and neon spectral halos on critical milestones).

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an abyss of deep slate-obsidian tones, engineered to completely prevent eye fatigue during grueling thousand-solve training sessions while maximizing optical pop for scramble reading and multi-channel telemetry.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#00FF87` - Electric Emerald):** The signature velocity color. Used exclusively for "Timer Armed", sub-target records, clean solves, and primary kinetic CTAs. Emits subtle phosphorescent glow states.
- **Secondary (`#00E5FF` - Hyper Cyan):** Secondary telemetry indicator, match connectivity, active room status, live opponent delta splits, and scramble sequence identifiers.
- **Tertiary (`#FF3366` - Laser Ruby):** Critical game alerts, penalties (+2s, DNF), dropped inputs, competitor disconnects, and rival overtake warnings. An auxiliary Amber (`#FFB800`) is reserved strictly for inspection count warning countdowns (8s and 12s marks).
- **Neutral (`#090D12` - Deep Obsidian):** The foundational substrate. Blended with sub-shades:
  - Base Obsidian: `#090D12`
  - Elevated Container / Glass Card: `#0F1620` (at 75% opacity)
  - Ghost Telemetry Outlines: `#1C293A`
  - Subdued Text / Scramble Neutral: `#7D8D9E`
  - High-Contrast Text: `#F1F5F9`

### Surface Application
Avoid solid primary background floods. Neon colors act as razor-thin energy currents—running borders, glowing monospaced digits, and status pip embers across pitch-black planes.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances three distinct roles:
1. **Space Grotesk** brings technical, structural character to competitive tournament headers, podium ranks, and arena titles.
2. **Geist** delivers crisp, unencumbered legibility for data readouts, participant handles, match logs, and configuration matrices.
3. **JetBrains Mono** powers the operational core of the platform: scramble notation sequences, millisecond chronometers, standard deviations, and round telemetry. 

### Typographic Rules
- **Scramble Legibility:** All WCA scramble notation (`R U R' U' F2 D B'`) must use `scramble-lg` or `scramble-sm` with tabular figures and uppercase tracking to prevent glyph collision during rapid visual inspection.
- **Timer Stacking:** The main inspection/solving chronometer strictly uses `display-timer` with tabular zero-slashed figures to eliminate horizontal jitter when milliseconds cycle at 1000 FPS.
- **Micro Labels:** All arena stats (Ao5, Ao12, Best, Deviation) are presented using `label-mono-xs` in full uppercase.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system operates on a rigid 4px spatial baseline rhythm designed for telemetry balance and high-density information display.

### Layout Philosophy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** A 12-column fluid grid. Layout organizes into a 3-zone command center: Left telemetry panel (Ao5/Ao12 trends, match queue, 3 cols), Center arena canvas (Scramble banner, primary stopwatch, cube state preview, 6 cols), and Right multiplayer arena standings/chat (3 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** An 8-column layout with collapsable drawer sheets for competitor standings and persistent center stopwatch.
- **Mobile (<768px):** A single vertical column stack with the timer occupying 45vh viewport real estate, bottom sheet drawer for live duel splits, and a pinned scramble header across the safe area top.

### Rules of Space
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for high-density tabular scoreboards, split differentials, and stat matrices.
- Scramble sequences must always maintain at least `space-lg` vertical clearance from the running timer display to guarantee distraction-free tracking during spacebar release.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built without heavy drop shadows. Depth is established through **tonal stacking, 1px glassmorphic borders, and controlled neon bioluminescence**.

### Tiers of Elevation
- **Level 0 (Arena Canvas):** Pure obsidian slate `#090D12` void. No reflections.
- **Level 1 (Data Modules & Panels):** `#0F1620` at 80% opacity with a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a subtle `1px` structural outline of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Scramble Cards):** `#141D2B` with a `1px` border of `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.25)` or `rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.25)` depending on solver readiness.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Match Found & Podium Overlays):** `#182232` with a `1px` perimeter border and an ambient, ultra-diffused phosphorescent perimeter shadow: `0 0 32px -8px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.15)`.

### Timer Energy States
- **Idle:** Border neutral subtle `#1C293A`, typography `#F1F5F9`.
- **Armed (Holding Spacebar):** Surface snaps to a faint ruby gradient flash `#FF3366` (0.05 opacity).
- **Ready (Green Light):** Instant border transition to `#00FF87` with an ambient glow of `0 0 24px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.20)`.
- **Timing:** All structural card borders drop to 0% opacity to eliminate visual distraction; digits render in stark, crisp contrast.

## Shapes

The design system favors crisp, mechanical precision with soft corner chamfers (`roundedness: 1` = 0.25rem / 4px base radius). 

### Corner Archetypes
- **Telemetry Cards, Stat Cells, & Scramble Blocks:** `rounded` (4px). Keeps data containers looking sharp, industrial, and tightly integrated into the grid.
- **Badges, Status Indicators, & Mode Pills:** Fully radiused pill shapes (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish ephemeral metadata from core structural containers.
- **Modal Dialogs & Podium Overlays:** `rounded-lg` (8px) for balanced framing of larger hero elements.
- **Geometry Rules:** Never use bubbly or exaggerated radii. Shapes should suggest precision digital hardware and tactical avionics.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Kinetic Button:** Dark slate substrate encased in a 1px `#00FF87` line with electric emerald text. On hover, the background transitions to `#00FF87` with `#090D12` obsidian text and a subtle `0 0 16px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.35)` glow.
- **Ghost Utility Button:** Borderless, neutral text (`#7D8D9E`), hovering to `#F1F5F9` with a subtle `#141D2B` rounded container fill.
- **Destructive / DNF Button:** Outlined in `#FF3366` with high-urgency ruby feedback on trigger.

### Scramble Banner
- Contained within a Level 1 glassmorphic slab. Notation split into clear token clusters with contrasting notation colors for turn layers (e.g., standard moves in `#F1F5F9`, double moves like `U2` highlighted in `#00E5FF`, prime inverse moves like `R'` in `#FFB800`).

### High-Speed Chronometer
- Scaled via `display-timer`. Integrates millisecond resolution `00:00.000`. The millisecond suffix is rendered at 60% font size of the primary integer seconds to establish immediate visual hierarchy.

### Leaderboards & Opponent Spliter Cards
- Row items use alternating slate zebra styling (`transparent` vs `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`).
- Time deltas (`+0.42`, `-1.18`) utilize `JetBrains Mono` with dynamic color tokens (`#00FF87` for positive delta leads, `#FF3366` for trailing splits).
- Personal best rows feature a left-aligned vertical 2px glowing primary accent track.

### Badges & Room Tags
- Pill-shaped tags with `0.5rem` horizontal padding and `0.15rem` vertical padding.
- Built using deep tinted backgrounds (e.g., `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.1)`) accompanied by solid 1px borders and high-luminance monospaced text.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- Inactive input surfaces sit flush at `#0F1620` with a 1px `#1C293A` border. On focus, the border transitions seamlessly to Hyper Cyan (`#00E5FF`) with zero drop shadows.
